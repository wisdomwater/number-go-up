## About the Author

Michael Collins is a software engineer, writer, and longtime student of the complicated relationship between people and systems.

After nearly three decades working in the technology industry, he has spent much of his career inside the kinds of organizations described in *Number Go Up*—building software, working with engineering teams, watching dashboards multiply, and occasionally wondering whether the numbers were helping everyone understand the work or simply giving them more numbers to discuss in meetings.

His interests extend beyond technology. Michael writes about spirituality, contemplation, and the ways people make meaning of their lives. That combination of engineering and contemplation shapes *Number Go Up*: a respect for measurement and evidence alongside an equally strong conviction that reality is always larger than our representations of it.

He lives in Northern California with his wife and continues to write about technology, spirituality, meaning, and the strange experience of being human in systems that would very much like everything to fit neatly into a spreadsheet.
